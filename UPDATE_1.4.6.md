# Target & Sales 1.4.6

- System administrators can correct an employee's package quantities for a selected business date; package units and points recalculate from the package details and the change is recorded in the audit log.
- System administrators can close an open shift for a selected business date. The close is recorded as an administrative closure and synchronized to attendance.
- Hotel activations now include a daily or monthly employee total, alongside existing hotel totals.
- Android build 32.
